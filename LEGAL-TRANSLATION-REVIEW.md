# Bulgarian legal translations — lawyer review required

The Bulgarian translations of the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are provided for language accessibility and have not been legally reviewed. Before publication, have them reviewed by qualified counsel familiar with Bangladesh law and Bulgarian-language legal drafting.

The source pages specify Bangladesh law and, for the Terms of Service, the competent courts of Dhaka, Bangladesh. The translations are intended to preserve that meaning; they must not be adapted to replace Bangladesh governing law or jurisdiction with Bulgarian or EU law. Review the Bulgarian text against the current English source before making legal or substantive changes.
