# Montenegro: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: sr

Sources:
- Government of Montenegro, press release of 108th Cabinet session, 18 Dec 2025 (gov.me/en/article/press-release-from-the-108th-cabinet-session): 2026 quota and sector split.
- Law on Foreigners text hosted by the Employment Agency (zzzcg.me/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Zakon-o-strancima.pdf), undated posting, 2018 text with amendments up to unknown date: articles 21, 40, 66-71, 76-85, 210 (deadlines, validity, fines).
- Ministry of Interior procedure sheet (psc.gov.me/media/c1sbe0sj/privremeni-boravak-i-rad-radna-dozvola.docx), dated 26 Apr 2024: EUR 67 fee, documents, 15 days, appeal.
- Employment Agency page, Uredba on quota criteria (zzzcg.me/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/...), OG 20/2019: quota criteria, no nationality limits, no share cap.
- Official gazette index, OG 33/2026 (sluzbenilist.me/propisi/391538), 10 Mar 2026: amendment in force 18 Mar 2026 (content not read).
- gov.me, 27 Jul 2026 (visa policy aligned with EU, VFS centres incl. Bangladesh); gov.me, 11 Apr 2025 (VFS pilot, Dhaka); gov.me Bangladesh mission page (visa required; VFS Dhaka address; non-resident Bangladeshi ambassador in Madrid).
- KPMG Montenegro tax alert, Oct 2024 (older than 6 months): minimum wage EUR 600/800 from 1 Oct 2024, employer pension contribution 0%.
- Secondary (non-official, used only for context and flagged): montenegrobusiness.eu (27 Jul and 18 Aug 2026) on planned single permit and 2025 permit statistics; investitor.me (24 Jul 2026) and standard.co.me (UPCG) on planned ZZZCG consent; UNB (Bangladesh news agency, 5 Oct 2026) on G2G talks; unija.com (19 Dec 2025) and ekonomik.co (15 Jan 2026) on the Dec 2025 amendments (not used in the guide body).

Not confident / not confirmed:
- Content of the 2025-2026 amendments to the Law on Foreigners (OG 33/2026 and the Dec 2025 amendment); the Law text consulted may be outdated, so deadlines, validity and fines should be rechecked.
- Order of D visa and permit application (Art. 79 is ambiguous when the worker is abroad); D visa fee, processing time, and whether embassies still accept D visa applications besides VFS Dhaka.
- Whether a labour market test or share-of-foreigners cap exists in current rules; minimum salary specific to foreigners; housing, working-time, medical check fees, health insurance cost, tax registration form.
- BMET side: no official page found for Montenegro (licensed agency requirement, Smart Card, demand letter attestation); not confirmed.
- Total timeline is an estimate only. Annotation: the MUP fee sheet is from April 2024 and fees may have changed.
