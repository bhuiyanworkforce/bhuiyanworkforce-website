# Singapore: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: en

Sources, all fetched 14 Nov 2026 (page update dates in brackets where shown):
- MOM Apply for a Work Permit: fees 35+35, steps, 14-day issuance, 1 week review.
- MOM Construction, Marine, Process requirements pages: Bangladesh as NTS, levies, quotas, ages, ASPRI conditions, 2028 levy rises.
- MOM NTS Occupation List (updated 1 Sep 2026): Bangladesh included, 8 percent sub-ceiling, 2,000 dollar salary, occupations.
- MOM COS 2026 factsheet (3 Mar 2026): new NTS occupations from Sep 2026, EP/S Pass changes.
- MOM Security bond page: 5,000 dollars. MOM Medical insurance page: 60,000 dollars per year.
- MOM Onboard centre page (28 Apr 2026): fees, durations. MOM press release 22 Apr 2022: PDPP in Bangladesh.
- MOM Local Qualifying Salary page (1 Jul 2026): 1,800 dollars. MOM EFMA page: penalties. MOM Work Permit conditions PDF (undated, older): 7-day salary rule, 5-day address rule.
- BCA Overseas Trade Testing page: 925 dollar cap, 2028 SEC(K) deadline.
- Singapore High Commission Dhaka visa page; Bangladesh High Commission Singapore 2023 attestation form.

Not confident or not confirmed:
- Whether a Work Permit worker from Bangladesh needs a visa beyond the IPA letter (ICA page silent).
- All Bangladesh-side rules: BMET clearance, Smart Card, mandatory licensed agency, fees. BMET and Bangladesh mission pages and the Act text were not available to check. No Big Four or law firm alert was found for this.
- Current PDPP status, security bond and insurance premiums, Bangladesh test centre availability, labour market test (none found), exact IPA validity.
- Laws cited without checking the current text: Employment Act 1968, Foreign Employee Dormitories Act 2015, Work Injury Compensation Act 2019, Employment Agencies Act 1958, regulations.
- The 15,000 dollar insurance figure comes from an older MOM document and conflicts with the 60,000 dollar page; the page was treated as authoritative.
- Total timeline is an estimate. Manufacturing and services quotas were not checked.
