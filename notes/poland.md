# Poland: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: pl

- Act of 20 March 2025, Dz. U. 2025 poz. 621 (api.sejm.gov.pl consolidated text, 2026-06-17 version): articles 5, 13, 15, 17, 19-20, 29-31, 59, 84; fines; validity; no general labour market test; caps possible under Art. 13. Read only partially (pages up to about char 200,000 of 223,538).
- PUP Gdansk and PUP Wroclaw pages (praca.gov.pl, undated, post 1 Dec 2025): declaration countries, fees, 7-day deadlines, ZUS/NFZ 7 days, labour market test abolished.
- Gazeta Prawna (press, undated): fees under regulation of 20 Nov 2025, fast track, 12-month rule, test abolished "from 2026".
- gov.pl/web/india pages (checked Nov 2026, undated): New Delhi jurisdiction for Bangladesh, 15/30 days, EUR 30,000 insurance, weekly draw, 14-day appeal. Notice of 14 April 2025: no agents.
- VFS India work checklist (undated, India-specific): documents.
- powroty.gov.pl: national visa fee EUR 200 from 1 Jan 2026 (regulation of 29 Oct 2025); page does not link it explicitly to type D.
- Swedish embassy Dhaka notice, 5 April 2026: last day 15 April 2026.
- EY alerts 6 July, 23 July 2026 and 14 April 2026 item: draft changes, not in force. MSWiA statistics via Mr East (24 Aug 2026, secondary).
- Minimum wage 2027: portaloswiatowy.pl article of 17 Sep 2026 (secondary); 2026 figure derived (4,950 minus 144).

Not confident or not confirmed:
- Voivode processing time; any cap or share limit applicable to Bangladeshis; shortage-occupation (fast track) list; exact start date of test abolition.
- Bangladesh side entirely (BMET clearance, Smart Card, demand letter attestation, licensed agency requirement for Poland): official pages could not be opened or did not cover it; the Act's name in Polish is a translation.
- Whether a VFS centre operates inside Bangladesh; whether the single residence-and-work permit can be used first.
- Pre-employment medical checks, BHP, working-time rules: Labour Code not checked against the text. Attachments required for the work permit (e.g. criminal record statement) were seen only for declarations.
- mos.cudzoziemcy.gov.pl, gov.pl visa pages and prawo.pl were not available to check.
