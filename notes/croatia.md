# Croatia: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: hr

Sources (dates as seen; "accessed 14-17 Nov 2026"):
- zakon.hr text of NN 55/2026 (27 May 2026): Aliens Act amendments, 20%/10% ratio, 100,000/40,000 EUR inflow, 30-day account blockage, 90+30 day decision, language test, accommodation notice 15 days, entry into force 8th day after publication, employer pays fee. narodne-novine.nn.hr was not available to check; text read via zakon.hr (secondary host of the official text).
- Sabor proposal PZE_262 (23 Apr 2026): same ratio/threshold provisions, agency fine EUR 1,500-3,000 per worker.
- zakon.hr NN 40/25 (7 Mar 2025): promissory note (Art. 29/95), 5 days, one average monthly gross salary, pay-in within 8 days; pay not lower than comparable workers; 5 working days to report contract end.
- AJ Law Firm article (30 Mar 2025): 2025 ratio 16%/8%, EUR 10,000/month turnover (superseded), fines EUR 5,000-20,000 / 2,000-6,000, 90 days after HZZ report, 3-year permits.
- MUP pages (mup.gov.hr/stranci and /radne-dozvole, seen Nov 2026): HZZ vs police routes, 90 days after test, language exam from 4 June 2027, work limited to police area, burzarada.hzz.hr.
- MVEP visa pages: long-stay D visa (EUR 93, EUR 30,000 insurance, 6 months validity, timing windows); visa requirements overview for Bangladesh (VFS Dhaka; New Delhi embassy contact; Dhaka office).
- Croatiaweek 21-27 Apr 2026 and Finacro 24 Apr 2026 (secondary): Pravilnik NN 43/2026 effective 30 Apr 2026, Forms 17a and 18a, 14 m2 / 6 m2 / 30% rent rules.
- Uredba o visini minimalne place za 2026 (NN 132/25): EUR 1,050 gross (via search listing and teb.hr).
- TBS News (28 Jan 2025), N1 Info (29 Jan 2025, citing Vecernji list), Croatiaweek (29 Jan 2025): reported Bangladesh suspension. These are news media, not official; used only because no official statement was found.
