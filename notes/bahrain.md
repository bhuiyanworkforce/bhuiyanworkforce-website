# Bahrain: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: ar

Sources:
- LMRA, New Work Permit page (lmra.gov.bh/en/page/show/106), accessed 14 Nov 2026: EMS, form name, documents, fees 195/390 + 5 + 30, 21 working days, residence permit via National Portal.
- LMRA, Commercial Services Fees (page/show/470): monthly fees 7.5/12.5, visit-to-work BHD 250, medical insurance column. The table layout was garbled in extraction.
- LMRA, Decision No. 76 of 2008 (legal/show/14; page last updated 17 Feb 2025): eligibility, 3 business days, 6-month void rule, 2-year validity, employer and worker duties.
- LMRA, Labour Law No. 36 of 2012 (English unofficial translation, first 100,000 characters only): contract, hours, leave, probation, wages, penalties.
- LMRA wages and indemnity booklet (undated): WPS, delay compensation, SIO penalties 5% and 20%.
- SIO, End of Service Gratuity for non-Bahrainis: 4.2% and 8.4%, effective 1 March 2024.
- LMRA domestic worker page and LMRA page/show/203: Wafid medical, non-Wafid form, airport biometrics.
- ILO, 17 Dec 2025: Bangladesh portals (Aspirant Migrant Portal, RAIMS, Employer and Mission Portal).
- Daily Star, 1-2 Nov 2025; 30 Mar 2026: Bahrain closed or restricted. Dhaka Tribune, 8 Jul 2026: reopening efforts ongoing. TBS News, 18 Jun 2026 and 13 Jul 2025.
- Law firm and press on the 21-day rule: DLA Piper (26 Mar 2026), News of Bahrain (14 Feb 2026), Visas Update (27-28 Mar 2026). Envoy Global (14 Oct 2026) on Resolution No. 2 of 2026.

Low confidence or not confirmed:
- Bahrain's status for Bangladeshis rests on Bangladeshi press, not on an official Bahraini or Bangladeshi government notice. The 2018 suspension date is press-only.
- Which embassy or centre handles Bangladeshi visa applications, and how the entry visa is issued, were not confirmed. The Bahrain eVisa and MOFA pages were not available to check.
- The 21-day rule has no confirmed decision number, effective date or exemptions, and no official LMRA or Ministry of Labour page was read. The 7-day advert and the 30 BHD advert fee may be linked, but this is not confirmed.
- Bangladesh-side items not confirmed: mandatory licensed agency for Bahrain, Smart Card, attestation of demand letter, power of attorney, and fees. The BMET site and the MOFA demand-letter template were not readable.
- Not confirmed: Wafid centre in Bangladesh, minimum salary, Bahrainisation percentages, shortage list, housing duty, labour-law fine amounts, and general SIO contribution rates for expatriates (only end-of-service contributions found).
- The fee table's 195 and 90 medical insurance figures are ambiguous. The 21 working days (LMRA page) conflicts with 3 business days (Decision 76); the LMRA page is treated as more current.
- The Labour Law text read was an unofficial English translation and truncated, so housing and repatriation provisions were not checked.
