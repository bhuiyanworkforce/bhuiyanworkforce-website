# Mauritius: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: en

Sources, fetched 17 Nov 2026:
- Ministry of Labour work permit page: section 3(1) of the Act, links to guidelines and forms.
- Ministry of Labour Guidelines for Work Permit Application (June 2016): eligibility, ratios 3:1 and 1:1, validity, fees, recruitment licence, Annex 1 medical requirements and Bangladeshi laboratories not accepted, LAP.
- Ministry of Labour Work Permit Information Sheet (September 2024, hosted on noubiznes.mu): PIP rule and exempt sectors, NELS, residence permit filed automatically from 1 April 2024, MUR 50,000 contract-vetting threshold, LAP, provisional health clearance, bus-driver sourcing countries.
- EDB NSDC guideline (May 2026): NELS use for Indian recruitment (shows NELS is current).
- BSS, Bangladesh Monitor, Prothom Alo (June 2026): ministers' meeting on 8 June 2026 and planned MoU.
- Dhaka Tribune (2024), New Age (2023): limited visas, 78,028 placements since 1992, 10 Bangladeshi agencies.
- Paul Hastings (2025): Finance Act 2025 changes (NELS, electronic permit, new employer duty).

Not confident or not confirmed:
- Whether the MoU has been signed, and what restrictions currently apply to Bangladeshi nationals specifically.
- Whether BOESL or only private agents recruit for Mauritius; BMET clearance, Smart Card and fees.
- Current work permit fees, local-worker ratios, the MUR 50,000 vetting threshold and the Bangladeshi laboratory list (the main fee and ratio figures are from the 2016 guidelines).
- Whether a separate entry visa is needed; official processing times; the new employer duty under the Finance Act 2025; penalties under the Act.
- Section 1's description of the market closure comes from Bangladeshi press, not from a Mauritian government notice.
- Whether work permit fees are one-off or yearly, and who pays the air ticket (the employer-pays principle comes from a secondary source).
- The exact title of the 2025 Act behind NELS and electronic permits.
- The total timeline is an estimate.
