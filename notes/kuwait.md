# Kuwait: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: ar

Sources (date, facts used):
- Bangladesh Embassy Kuwait homepage https://kuwait.mofa.gov.bd/ (accessed Nov 2026): contacts, attestation emails. The visa-attestation subpages were not available to check; only their titles were seen in search results.
- TBS, 2 Jul 2026: embassy statement on no MoU for female domestic workers, no attestation of Khadama visas.
- TBS, 17 Apr 2026: BMET cleared 1,539 Kuwait workers in March; visas almost stopped; airport closed.
- TBS, 1 Mar 2025: embassy-authorised agencies, Tk6,270 fee, alleged extra payments, visa trading.
- TBS, 3 Feb 2025: no bilateral manpower agreement; about 280,000 Bangladeshis in Kuwait.
- TBS, 1 Dec 2024: BMET card fee scrapped (notice 25 Nov 2024).
- Fragomen, 15 Jan 2026: residence reforms, visa and residency fees, visit-visa conversion.
- Deloitte, 2 Jul 2025: exit permit from 1 Jul 2025.
- KPMG Middle East Immigration Tracker (Mar 2026) and KPMG Flash Alert 2026-131 (26 May 2026): PAM then MOI process; Wafid bylaw.
- Arab Times, 25 May 2026, and 16 Apr 2026 report: Ministerial Resolution 2/2026 temporary sector transfers (1 May-30 Jun 2026, expired).
- bwtravel (9 Jun 2025) and secondary summaries: Ministerial Resolution 4/2025 KD150 fee.
- Kuwait Times, 6 Mar 2018 (old): the 2018 Article 20 visa ban for Bangladeshis; Kuwait Times 27 Jul 2026: PAM chief met Bangladesh ambassador (no details).
- Law 6/2010 article details come from an explanatory-memorandum PDF hosted by Pakistan's BEOE, not the Kuwaiti official gazette.

Weak or not confirmed:
- The PAM, MOI and BMET websites were not available to check, so no rule was checked on the primary portals. The PAM URL was not verified.
- Whether Article 18 visa issuance for Bangladeshis has normalised since April 2026: no source found after that date. Also whether Kuwait or Bangladesh impose any current restriction on new demand letters.
- Fees from non-official or secondary sources: health insurance KD 130, transfer fee, Wafid fee. Work permit validity (sources conflict: 1-3 years). General private-sector minimum wage (sources conflict, KD 60/75 not verified). Kuwaitization quotas, labour market test, foreign-worker share caps.
- Bangladesh-side: Overseas Employment and Migrants Act 2013 not cited in guide as not re-verified; whether a BMET-licensed agency is mandatory for Kuwait; demand letter attestation details; whether direct visa applications at the Kuwait Embassy in Dhaka are possible.
- Several fee amounts (KD 10 per month entry visa, KD 20 residency) are Fragomen's reading of the new rules; effective date not stated.
- The 2-4 month total timeline is our estimate.
