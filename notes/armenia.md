# Armenia: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: en

Sources used:
- Migration and Citizenship Service, notice on mcs-permits.am and workpermit.am, 13 Nov 2026: platform switch on 1 Nov, no new applications on workpermit.am.
- Migration and Citizenship Service, notice of 19 Jun 2026: digitisation from 1 Nov 2026; page "Residency status and visa" (undated): invitation for 57 countries, 14 Tairov Street, hartak.am, employer online filing since 2022, phone 8422.
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs, invitation-only list (updated 2 Feb 2024): Bangladesh is no. 3. Visa page (undated): AMD 5,000 invitation fee, e-request.am.
- Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Bangladesh bilateral page: ambassador based in New Delhi, 1992 protocol.
- Embassy of Armenia in India, FAQ (undated): Bangladeshis need an invitation and apply abroad.
- Fragomen alert, 10 Mar 2026: work visa AMD 15,000, quota, labour test removed, 15-business-day notice. Older than six months.
- EY Armenia alert, 17 Feb 2026: law adopted 20 Jan 2026, fees AMD 150,000 and 250,000, quota. Older than six months.
- PAN.am news, 31 Oct 2026: 30-day decision, 183-day notice. News agency, not the legal text.
- MOSTAR and armenian-lawyer.com (law-firm commentary, last updated 28 Oct and 11 Nov 2026): work visa waits for the e-visa system, fees AMD 155,950, old salary AMD 75,000, old steps, digital contract. Not official.

Points not confirmed:
- Employer filing steps and form names on mcs-permits.am; the notice does not describe them.
- Work visa start date, quota size, new salary threshold; fee discrepancy (150,000 vs 155,950).
- Which Armenian mission accepts Bangladeshi applications (New Delhi is accredited; direct acceptance not verified).
- Bangladesh-side rules for Armenia (BMET registration, Smart Card, licensed agency, demand-letter attestation): BMET's current rules were not available to check; the Act 2013 reference was not checked against the text.
- Medical check, tax and social registration steps, minimum wage, working hours, insurance, penalties, original law year, contract language.
- Bangladesh was not on the official e-visa pages read; the evisa.mfa.am page did not list nationalities.
