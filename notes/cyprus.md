# Cyprus: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: el

Sources used:
- CRMD entry permit page, moi.gov.cy (undated): entry permit mandatory for work; applications only in Nicosia; fees EUR 120/130/70; 4-month decision period; biometrics; 3-year permit, 4-year max stay; 30-day rule after mutual release.
- CRMD submission page, moi.gov.cy (undated): biometrics, 4-month examination; fees for a different category (family/sponsor route, EUR 80/70), not used for this guide.
- CRMD employment FAQ page, moi.gov.cy (undated): work only for employer stated on permit; visitor permit does not allow work; renewal at least one month before expiry.
- Department of Labour page, mlsi.gov.cy (undated): Council of Ministers decision 33.210 (15/3/1990); local-labour-unavailability condition.
- Cyprus Mail, 27 Sep 2026 (press, not official): Bangladeshis among main general-category nationalities; Aug 2026 permit numbers.
- Cyprus Mail, 23 Dec 2025 (press quoting minister): minimum wage EUR 1,088/979 from 1 Jan 2026 to 31 Dec 2027.
- PIO and CNA, 28-29 Mar 2025 (official/press): High Commissioner in New Delhi accredited to Bangladesh.
- EEAS consular contacts list 2026: only Cyprus mission listed is New Delhi.
- VFS Global Cyprus-India page (undated): no Bangladesh centre; Bangladesh only on airport transit list.
- Cyprus Mail 19 Nov 2022 and TBS News 22 Nov 2022: return agreement; honorary consul quote (old).
- Law-firm pages: gk-lawfirm.com (updated 20 Oct 2026; stamp duty abolished from 1 Jan 2026, online-only renewals from 1 Jan 2027, 7-day registration, BFU route); Hadjivangeli (26 May 2025, older than 6 months; Feb 2025 sector ratios); Legal 500 2026 guide (30%/50% ratios, 4-6 week labour test, 3-4 months overall).

Not confident / not confirmed:
- Ratios of foreign staff (30/50/75/100%): only secondary, partly older than 6 months, internally inconsistent; no official source reached.
- Where and whether Bangladeshis need a visa stamp after entry-permit approval; New Delhi High Commission role for Bangladesh work cases.
- All Bangladesh-side rules (BMET registration, Smart Card, emigration clearance, mandatory licensed agency, demand-letter attestation) for Cyprus.
- Labour Department fees, vacancy advertising duration, form names (e.g. M-numbered forms), medical exam details, social insurance/tax registration steps, contract language, housing, working time, penalties.
- Official CRMD pages were not available to check for the general-category checklist, nor were gov.cy and mfa.gov.cy. The cost sum of EUR 320 assumes the three fees are cumulative.
- Schengen accession status of Cyprus.
- Guide wording "Δημόσιο δεν επιβάλλει πλέον χαρτόσημο" rests on a single law-firm source contradicting the department checklist.
