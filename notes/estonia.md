# Estonia: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: et

Sources:
- PPA, Residence permit for employment, politsei.ee (fetched Nov 2026): purposes, salary table from 5.3.2026, 90+30 day processing, 5-year validity, A2 language rule.
- PPA, Residence permit for employment, for employers; Working in Estonia, for employers (Nov 2026): Töötukassa permission, invitation form, address, notification duties, 30-day job-change review.
- Estonian Embassy New Delhi, pages for Bangladesh/Nepal/Sri Lanka citizens and where to submit Schengen applications (Nov 2026): German embassy Dhaka handles Estonian Schengen short-stay visas; D-visa and residence permit by appointment only at broneering.mfa.ee.
- EURES Estonia, Hiring a foreigner (Nov 2026): Töötukassa permission before PPA application; EURES free.
- Fragomen alert, 10 Jun 2026: reforms in force 22 May 2026; shortage-sector measure not in force, expected early 2027, list unpublished; fines up to EUR 100,000.
- KPMG Estonia, March 2026, and EAKL (5 May 2026): draft contents (0.8 wage, 0.2%/0.1% cap, fines).
- EY tax alert, June 2024: fees effective 1 Jan 2025.
- ERR News, 10 Nov 2025 (2026 quota 1,292) and 3 Nov 2026 (2027 draft quota 2,569): news media, not official or law-firm.
- Prothom Alo, 5 Nov 2026: BMET clearance list of 12 countries excludes Estonia; newspaper, not an official source.

Points of low or no confidence:
- The Töötukassa page (tootukassa.ee, English and Estonian) was not available to check; vacancy advertising period, processing time, fee, and exact conditions not confirmed.
- Whether the Estonian embassy in New Delhi actually accepts Bangladeshi D-visa/residence permit applications: not confirmed (page is ambiguous; no VFS found).
- All Bangladesh-side requirements (BMET, Smart Card, demand letter attestation, licensed agency): not confirmed; no official BMET or Estonian source found.
- 2026 state fees not reconfirmed from PPA (fee page not available to check); relied on 2024 EY alert. Salary currency not stated by PPA.
- Employment Contracts Act, minimum wage, working time, housing, insurance, medical check, EMTA/employment register registration: not researched from official sources; kept as not confirmed. Riigi Teataja was not available to check, so the Aliens Act section numbers were not verified.
- Two guide statements are our inferences: no Estonian embassy in Dhaka (inferred from German representation) and the roughly 4-month figure (sum of PPA maximums).
