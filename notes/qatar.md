# Qatar: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: ar

Sources used:
- ILO, Qatar adopts a non-discriminatory minimum wage (2020): QAR 1,000 / 300 / 500; Law 17 of 2020 adopted 30 Aug 2020.
- ILO, Overview of Qatar's labour reforms: 14 Qatar Visa Centers; NOC removed; heat rules.
- ILO, Ensuring fair recruitment through QVC Sri Lanka (18 June 2019): QVC process; employer pays.
- ILO FAIRWAY factsheet (Dec 2023): recruitment fees banned, passport penalty QAR 25,000, WPS, hours, heat.
- ILO, From registration to return (Bangladesh OEP): Aspirant Migrant Portal, RAIMS, Employer and Mission Portal.
- BSS News and TBS News (18 May 2026), reporting the 7th Joint Committee: four trades, five training centres, one QVC in Dhaka. Press, not official; Qatar Embassy in Dhaka page confirms the meeting only.
- Clyde & Co alert (July 2026): Law 9 of 2026 content, no effective date. Crowell alert (2026): non-compete, agency penalties QAR 3,000-25,000, 23 violations. LexisNexis note (July 2026).
- Clyde & Co staff mobilisation guide (Feb 2022): step timings, QVC countries. KPMG Middle East Immigration Tracker (March 2026): indicative fees. K&L Gates Qatar Employer Guide (undated): penalties, Qatarisation, QVC countries including Bangladesh.
- CRS News Flash (17 Sept 2026, secondary): Law 9/2026 effective 25 July 2026; Ministerial Decision 50 of 2026.

Not confident or not confirmed:
- All Hukoomi, MOL, MOI and QVC official pages were not available to check. Portal and form names, and the official fee schedule, are unverified.
- Law 9 of 2026 effective date (25 July 2026 from a single secondary source) and Decision 50 of 2026 (secondary only).
- Law number 19 of 2020 for the amendment of Law 21 of 2015 (from ILO translation title, not checked).
- Processing times are from 2022 and may be outdated; the 4-8 week total is our estimate.
- BMET Smart Card, mandatory use of licensed agency, attestation and power-of-attorney requirements: Bangladesh embassy and BMET pages not available to check.
- Tax and social-insurance registration for expatriate workers; labour market test; foreign-worker share cap; skilled-salary threshold: not found.
- Law 21 of 2015 penalty figures come from the K&L Gates guide, not the law text.
