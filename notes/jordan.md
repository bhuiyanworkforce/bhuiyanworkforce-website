# Jordan: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: ar

Sources used:
- Petra (Jordan News Agency), "Labour Ministry Freezes Foreign Worker Recruitment...", 4 June 2026: suspension, effective 1 June 2026, exemptions, existing approvals valid, no end date.
- Jordan Times, "Gov't suspends recruitment of non-Jordanians across most sectors" (undated in fetch; 2026): same decision, spokesperson Mohammad Al-Zyoud.
- EY Tax Alert, 15 June 2026: confirms suspension effective 1 June 2026 (exemptions not in the fetched text).
- Petra, 14-16 June 2026 (also Erickson Immigration Group, 16 June 2026): regularization 15 June-30 Sept 2026, 50% fee waiver, transfer limits, post-25 Feb 2025 recruits not transferable, Law No. 24 of 1973.
- Hammouri & Partners newsletter No. 61, October 2025: Amending Regulation No. 31 of 2025 on work permit fees; JD 350 / 225 / 800 / 700; JD 50 levy; fine at least twice fees; 50% late penalty after 90 days. (Slightly older than 6 months but a primary-law summary.)
- MoL, Instructions for 2012 and amendments (undated PDF): application form, approval validity 2/4 months, bank guarantee scale, contract, medical, housing, ratios.
- MoL, Comprehensive Guide of Principles for Issuing Work Permits (undated; latest internal date June 2023): closed professions, 30% Jordanian share, garment section, fees, JD 10 audit fee, JD 100 replacement, domestic worker exclusions.
- Jordan government portal, "Obtaining a Work Permit" (undated): documents list, old fees JD 180/300, 5% stamp, 15 minutes.
- Fragomen alert, 23 January 2023 (older than 6 months): SSC enrolment before permit.
- Daily Star, 21 November 2020 (news, not official): no Jordanian embassy in Dhaka at that time.
- BOESL notice titles (search results only): Jordan garment recruitment exists.

Not confirmed or weak:
- Which Jordanian mission or visa centre handles Bangladeshi visa applications today, and whether Bangladesh is a "restricted nationality" under the Residence Law (decides JD 300 guarantee vs scale, and 2 vs 4 month approval).
- All Bangladesh-side procedure for Jordan (BMET registration, Smart Card, demand letter attestation, mandatory agency/BOESL, fee ceilings): the BOESL pages, the probashi PDF and MoL Regulation No. 63 of 2020 were not available to check.
- Current minimum wage, labour market test (advertising period), contract language rule, working-time numbers, insurance amount, income tax registration, garment ratios (source text ambiguous), current Social Security law reference and rate.
- Total timeline is our estimate only. The JD 350 fee vs the portal's JD 300 is resolved in favour of the 2025 regulation, but the 5% stamp and JD 2 certification fee are unverified after 2025.
- Whether the 1 June 2026 suspension has been changed since mid-June 2026; no later official notice found.
