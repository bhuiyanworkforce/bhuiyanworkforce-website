# Saudi Arabia: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: ar

Sources (date, facts taken):
- HRSD Labor Law PDF (hrsd.gov.sa, 2023 upload of M/51 of 2005): Articles 9, 33, 36, 40, 53; fines articles shown as repealed.
- HRSD news, effective date of Labor Law amendments (announced 26 Feb 2025): effective 19 Feb 2025; Cabinet Decision 117 and Royal Decree 44 of 1446H.
- HRSD awareness guide on rights and duties (Dec 2025 upload): hours, Ramadan hours, overtime, probation, housing, transport, insurance.
- HRSD work permit service page: SAR 100, via Qiwa and Sadad.
- HRSD levy document (Resolution 325 of 1442H, uploaded 2021/2022): SAR 700/800 monthly, 9,100/8,400 for 12 months.
- HRSD visa issuance service page: free, immediate, for high-Saudization compliant firms.
- HRSD news, 3 May 2026: Nitaqat based on Qiwa contracts from 15 April 2026.
- KPMG GMS Flash Alerts 2026-116 (27 Apr), 2026-155 (22 Jun), 2026-157 (23 Jun), 2026-188 (22 Jul): contract compliance 85%/90%, premium residency permit, instant visa caps and conditions, nationality diversity limits.
- PwC GCC employment updates 2026: unified contract phases 6 Oct 2025, 6 Mar 2026, 6 Aug 2026.
- GOSI employer FAQ: 2% occupational hazards, registration by the 15th, fines up to SAR 5,000 and SAR 50,000.
- Wafid FAQ: USD 10 booking fee, Bangladesh covered.
- Non-official press (used only for context and flagged in the guide): TBS News 7 Mar 2026 (visa curbs, Tk165,000 cap), TBS 14 Nov 2025 (Takamol USD 50 test), Bonik Barta 2 Jul 2025 (skill certificate from 19 Jun 2025, 33 occupations), Daily Star 10 Oct 2023 (Tasheer biometrics), Prothom Alo 1 May 2023 (e-visa).

Low confidence or not confirmed:
- Which office actually takes Bangladeshi visa applications today: the Saudi embassy page and the Tasheer pages were not available to check; Tasheer and e-visa facts are 2023 press.
- Whether the March 2026 visa curbs still apply; no official statement found.
- Entire Bangladesh side (BMET, smart card, clearance, mandatory agency, attestation): BMET and Bangladesh mission pages were not available to check; nothing confirmed from official pages.
- Standard (non-instant) visa fee, iqama fee and deadline, medical exam fee, health insurance cost, minimum wage, closed-occupation list, labour market test, foreign-share cap, Labor Law penalty amounts.
- Skill certificate scope (33 occupations, USD 50) is press-based only. The levy table in the ministry PDF is internally inconsistent (800 x 12 = 9,600 vs 9,100) and may be superseded. Annual leave of 21 days was not checked against the Labor Law text or the sources; the guide consulted only stated 30 days after five years.
- Time estimate of 2 to 4 months is our own estimate.
