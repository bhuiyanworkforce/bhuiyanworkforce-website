# North Macedonia: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: mk

Sources:
- Assembly decree 08-4867/1 of 12 Sep 2025 (text of amendments to the Law on Foreigners, hosted at mchamber.mk): Art. 83-86 changes (notarised guarantee, 7-day completion, 30-day decision, extensions 90 to 5 days, strategic partners).
- EY tax alert, 30 Sep 2025: amendments effective 26 Sep 2025 (older than 6 months).
- LB Law (lblaw.com.mk) quota article, Jan 2026, and izvoz.mk, 16 Jan 2026: quota numbers and dates (secondary; the decision text itself was not read).
- av.gov.mk "Вработување на странци" page, read Nov 2026: ESA gives opinion; MVR issues permit; ESA work permit only for already-resident foreigners.
- cms.mtsp.gov.mk: appeal 8 days, Ministry decides in 15 days.
- mfa.gov.mk visa procedure page, read Nov 2026: D visa EUR 70, 6 months to 15 days, appeal 8 days. Visa-waiver decision valid 1 Jan to 31 Dec 2026.
- CoE GRETA annex (Law on Employment and Work of Foreigners): 5% cap, Art. 6 priorities, fines, deadlines. Older consolidated version; may be outdated.
- telma.com.mk, 16 Jan 2026 (media): Bangladesh 2nd with 1,567 positive opinions in 2025; used only as context, not in the guide.

Not confident or not confirmed:
- Whether Bangladesh requires a visa and which mission handles applications (the mfa.gov.mk Asia list was not available to check).
- All BMET / Bangladesh-side requirements; no official source found.
- ESA and MVR fees, document lists, processing times, labour market test length, minimum salary, minimum wage, health insurance, residence card, and total timeline.
- Gazette number of the 2025 amendment; whether the penalties and one-day registration rule are unchanged in the current law; no law-firm alert from the last 6 months was found; the "new law on foreigners" is only reported, not verified.
- Wording "Закон за работните односи" and "Визен центар" naming were not verified against a primary source.
