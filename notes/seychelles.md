# Seychelles: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: en

Sources, fetched 17 Nov 2026:
- Immigration and Civil Status GOP page: approval first, application one week before start, fees SCR 1,000 and 500 a month, collection 1 to 5 days, no entry before the GOP.
- Employment Department labour migration FAQ: advert and local-first test, documents, 3 to 9 working days, appeal 7 working days, attestation SCR 750 within one month, penalties SCR 20,000, 40,000, 400 a day, Prohibited Immigrant rules.
- Employment Department press releases (Oct 2018 moratorium; Oct 2019 labour agreement and procedures): BOESL and BMET only, Smart Card, entry documents, employer costs, accommodation, Registrar of Companies.
- Seychelles News Agency, 25 Sep 2026: new National Labour Migration Policy and quota review.
- US State Department 2026 Trafficking in Persons Report: Seychelles Tier 2, weak oversight of recruiters.
- Teamed hiring guide (22 Oct 2026, secondary): SCR 150 approval fee, fixed-term contract rule, Employment Act amendment 2025.
- Daily Star, Financial Express (2021), New Age (2019): BMET/BOESL channel, zero worker cost, 2021 first flights.

Not confident or not confirmed:
- Whether the 2019 labour agreement was renewed after its four-year term, and whether BOESL/BMET are still the only channel in November 2026.
- Whether the consular endorsement of the GOP in Dhaka is still required, and whether an entry visa or visitor's permit is needed on arrival.
- Medical tests and approved centres; BMET emigration clearance, welfare fees and the BOESL/BMET service charge.
- Open sectors, quota numbers, and the effect of the 2025 Employment Act amendment and the new migration policy.
- The current minimum wage figure (removed from the text) and whether it applies in full to GOP holders.
- Whether the contract must be attested before the worker starts or within one month of starting.
- Fee for approval of post (SCR 150) and the 2019 policy items come from secondary or older sources.
- The total timeline is an estimate.
