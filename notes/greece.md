# Greece: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: el

Sources (accessed 14-16 Nov 2026):
- mitos.gov.gr seasonal agriculture page (Art. 63): fee EUR 100 (code 2113), 10 days, documents, JMD 8/95931/2024, appeal EUR 50. Undated.
- mitos.gov.gr E.4 employer approval page: EUR 200 (code 2114), 50-60 days, income thresholds, JMD 7/95931/2024. Undated; still shows 1-year contract (conflicts with Law 5275/2026, which is more authoritative).
- mitos.gov.gr E.4 initial residence permit page (Greek): EUR 150 + 16, 50-80 days. Undated.
- Law 5275/2026, FEK A 17/6.2.2026 (migration.gov.gr PDF): Arts. 17, 18, 21, 45, 69; 6-month contract, EUR 200/EUR 400 fees, 30/15-day consular deadlines, age 18, TEA capital. Read only to offset 200,000 of the file; later articles unread.
- Cabinet Act 35/27.11.2025, FEK A 241/29.12.2025 (ypergasias.gov.gr PDF, via Jan 2026 upload): quotas, Bangladesh 4,000 / 9,000 allocation, 10% flex. Note: Law 5275 Art. 17 says 15% for future acts; the 2026 Act says 10%.
- bd-gr.gvcworld.eu (official external visa provider): Dhaka application, New Delhi processing, fees, passport and police certificate rules; notice of 17 March 2025 on India centres. Checklist PDF undated.
- mfa.gr India visas page: confirms New Delhi embassy accredited to Dhaka.
- PwC Legal Flash Feb 2026 and Zeya newsletter (law-firm alerts, older than 6 months, used only to corroborate Law 5275/2026).
- MedMA publication (academic, not official, Nov 2025): MoU background, Law 4959/2022, seasonal route "unimplemented". Minimum wage EUR 920 from 1 Apr 2026 (MD 8934/2026) from law-firm alert summaries only; the page itself was not checked.

Not confirmed or weak:
- Whether the MoU seasonal route is actually operating in 2026 and whether any government-to-government procedure applies; no official Greek or Bangladeshi source found.
- All Bangladesh-side steps (BMET, Smart Card, emigration clearance, demand letter attestation, licensed agency requirement) and visa processing time.
- ERGANI II and EFKA registration steps, penalty amounts, working-time rules, contract language, vacancy advertisement (DYPA) and share-of-foreign-workers limits; income thresholds and fees on mitos pages may predate Law 5275/2026; minimum wage figure; passport validity conflict (visa-centre notice says 1 year 6 months, checklist says 3 months beyond visa); whether a change of purpose from tourist status is possible; whether Greece has no embassy in Dhaka (inferred, not stated by an official page).
