# Hungary: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: hu

Sources (all accessed 14-15 Nov 2026):
- OIF news item on the guest-worker change (oif.gov.hu/hirek/modosult-a-vendegmunkas-tartozkodasi-engedelyekre-vonatkozo-szabalyozas), last edited 13 Nov 2026: Decree 92/2026 effective 6 June 2026, no new guest-worker permits, KKM notice 2/2025 revoked, pending cases rule (fee paid by 5 June), Resolution 1153/2026 review.
- Decree 450/2024 consolidated text (njt.jog.gov.hu), version of 6 June 2026: Annex 1 = Georgia, Armenia, Philippines.
- OIF page on employment-purpose permit: eligibility, 21/70-day limits, 2-year validity, 8-day appeal, D visa 30 days/3 months.
- OIF EU Blue Card page (2026): salary thresholds, 6-month contract, 4-year validity, Enter Hungary, 5-day end-of-employment notice.
- OIF fee page: HUF 26,000/39,000; EUR 110 visa fee.
- OIF National Card country page (last edited 15 Nov 2024): Annex 1 of Decree 179/2024, no Bangladesh. OIF combined procedure page: 8-day specialist opinion for Blue Card.
- OIF employer notice: 5-day notifications, HUF 1,000,000 fine, HUF 5,000,000 departure fine, joint liability.
- Decree 426/2025 (njt.jog.gov.hu): minimum wage HUF 322,800 and guaranteed minimum HUF 373,200 from 1 Jan 2026.
- Hungarian Embassy New Delhi pages (delhi.mfa.gov.hu): Bangladesh served from New Delhi, Dhaka Office handles D visas, Switzerland represents Hungary for C visas, employment D visa appointments not given for non-Annex countries.
- NAV page (dated 17 Mar 2023): T1041 registration before work starts.
- Deloitte Hungary alert (5 June 2026), WTS Klient (10 June 2026), Penzcentrum (13 Nov 2026, secondary, for the review plans of 1 Sept 2026), kormany.hu news (undated).

- Visa fee for a Blue Card applied for abroad (EUR 110 is stated for a visa tied to a residence permit; the Blue Card line was not explicit).

- Whether seasonal or investment-project guest-worker permits are open to Bangladeshis: not confirmed.
