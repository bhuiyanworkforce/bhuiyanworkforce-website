# Slovenia: sources and confidence (internal, not published)

Guide language: sl

Sources (all accessed 14-17 Nov 2026 unless stated):
- SPOT (spot.gov.si) single permit page: employer may apply; fee EUR 70; form fees EUR 15.47 / 26.51; SI-PASS login. Undated.
- ZRSZ (ess.gov.si) consent page: employer conditions, PDM-KTD (5 working days), documents, 30-day insurance deadline, 15-day decision for changes. Undated.
- ZRSZ fast-track page: Ordinance (OG 102/25 and amendments), changes from 21 Nov 2025; 10-day start; 80-hour language course; fine EUR 3,000-30,000. Only for foreigners already lawfully resident.
- Infotujci: procedure, documents, 15-day contract rule, 10-day fast-track, validity up to 2 years / 3 total. Undated.
- gov.si Embassy New Delhi visa page: Bangladesh needs visa; C visa via Sweden in Dhaka; D visa fee EUR 77, in person, appointment, EUR 30,000 insurance. Undated.
- gov.si Bangladesh representations: honorary consul in Dhaka without consular competence.
- Police (policija.si) D visa leaflet: D visa to be obtained before entry; apply at mission abroad. Undated.
- gov.si, 28 Jan 2026: minimum wage EUR 1,481.88; KPMG flash alert 2026-050, 3 Mar 2026 (same figure).
- EY tax alert (2025) and Kirm Perpar article, 30 May 2025: ZTuj-2I and ZZSDT-E; 21 May 2025 validity change; Blue Card salary threshold. Both are older than 6 months, used as supporting only.
- Mojpogled (media, not official) 22 Aug 2026: ZTuj-2J draft, 20 Aug 2026, Q4 2026 expected. Weak source.
- Sweden Abroad, Dhaka, 1 Nov 2021: representation agreement with Slovenia resumed; visa type not stated.

Not confirmed or weak:
- Which mission accepts D visa and single permit applications from Bangladeshis (New Delhi page does not name Bangladesh; Sweden representation is from 2021 and for unspecified visa type).
- BMET rules (Smart Card, emigration clearance, agency requirement, attestation): BMET rules were not available to check; nothing Slovenia-specific.
- Quota, share limit on foreign workers, vacancy advertising period, average gross wage figure, processing times, medical check, tax registration, working time and general penalties: not found in official sources.
- ZZSDT start date and the date of the ZRSZ brochure (Blue Card 1.5x vs average wage conflict).
- Cost and 3-6 month timeline are our estimates. The ZZSDT/ZTuj-2 primary texts were not checked.
